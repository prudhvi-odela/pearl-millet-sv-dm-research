# GWAS feasibility decision

Decision: NO-GO for a confirmatory genome-wide association claim at this stage; CONDITIONAL GO only for a clearly labelled exploratory/pilot SNP scan after the prespecified gates below. Independent sample size is 231, not 2,772. Phenotype ratios lack denominators and are boundary-heavy; no genotype QC, PCs, kinship or variant MAF distribution has been calculated.

A simple best-case power calculation at 88.8M marker-by-environment tests (22.2M markers x four sites) and N=231 implies an additive effect of roughly 0.66 phenotype SD per allele at MAF 0.5 and about 1.50 SD at MAF 0.05 for 80% power under independent normal residuals. This ignores population structure, missingness and repeated-site uncertainty, so real power is lower. See statistical_power_assessment.tsv. The cohort can plausibly detect only large effects in an exploratory scan. No VCF was downloaded and no GWAS was run.
