# Final research gap and publication readiness report

**Scope:** five candidates only: PGLA1542300, PGLA1557868, PGLA1564064, PGLA1457537, PGLA1545539.

## Main finding
All five are previously catalogued SVs with external GWAS/context evidence for traits other than downy mildew. No exact-ID direct DM or climate association was found in the reviewed candidate tables. They should be described as prioritized candidates, not novel SVs or validated resistance variants.

The local matrix has only 20 accessions, no matched local DM phenotype and no structure covariates; PGLA1557868 has no carriers. A public 2026 dataset has repeated DM phenotype measurements for 288 accessions and WGS for 231, but strict alias matching to the reviewed legacy accession aliases found no exact overlap, and the public processed VCFs do not contain verified PAV calls for these PI537069 IDs.

## Decisions
Climate adaptation has not been tested. Downy mildew resistance has not been demonstrated for these five exact SVs. Existing literature does contain pearl millet DM genetics and GWAS, so the gap is candidate-specific, not that the disease is unstudied. Current evidence is suitable for a transparent portfolio/interim report, not a credible standalone association paper.

## Best next analysis
Resolve the authoritative sample crosswalk for the 2026 231-accession WGS panel and call the exact five PAV alleles with reference/build-aware methods. Then establish phenotype overlap, carrier counts, population structure/kinship and site/replicate design before deciding on a mixed model and independent validation.
