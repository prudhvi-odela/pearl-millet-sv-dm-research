# References

The TSV evidence matrix (`literature_evidence_matrix.tsv`) records the specific scope used in the project audit. The following primary sources/data records are central to interpreting the claims:

- Yan et al. (2023), pearl millet pangenome and trait association study. DOI: [10.1038/s41588-023-01302-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01302-4). Candidate trait context does not imply downy-mildew evidence.
- Subedi et al. (2026), public pearl millet multi-site phenotype and WGS cohort data descriptor. DOI: [10.1016/j.dib.2026.112746](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2026.112746). Dataset record provides cohort context; this repository's exact join and audit are described in the reports.
- Ramu et al. (2023), improved pearl millet genome and breeding-panel GWAS, including DM resistance MTAs. DOI: [10.1038/s41477-023-01420-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-023-01420-9).
- Jones et al. (1995), pearl millet DM resistance QTL and pathogen-population context. DOI: [10.1007/BF00222972](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00222972).
- Saritha et al. (2017), pearl millet DM resistance QTL. [ICRISAT repository record 10458](https://oar.icrisat.org/10458/).
- Chelpuri et al. (2019), pearl millet DM resistance QTL across pathotype isolates. DOI: [10.1007/s10658-019-01718-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10658-019-01718-x).
- Taunk et al. (2018), marker-assisted introgression of DM resistance loci. DOI: [10.1007/s12041-018-1016-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12041-018-1016-7).

Use the primary papers and official dataset records for publication citations. `bibliography_research_opportunities.tsv` and `source_manifest_research_opportunities.tsv` are retained as provenance for the broader opportunity-selection exercise reflected in project history; they are not all specific to the pearl millet analyses.
